Awareness Timer

A tiny floating stopwatch for your sit/stand rhythm.

What it is: A minimal always-on-top timer that floats above everything you're working on and counts how many minutes have elapsed since you last reset it.

Why: Sitting for long stretches is easy to do without noticing. Keeping a small live counter in view makes you aware of your time in one posture, so you can decide to stand up, stretch, or switch — then tap reset and start the next block.

Opens a tiny always-on-top window. Requires Chrome or Edge.
